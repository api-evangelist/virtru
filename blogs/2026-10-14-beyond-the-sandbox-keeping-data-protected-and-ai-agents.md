Recent Axios reporting and an essay by Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella highlight a growing concern: AI agents are advancing faster than the controls designed to contain them. To be useful, agents need access to tools, networks, and data, but that same access can enable unintended actions, even without a sandbox escape. At Virtru, we believe this challenge demands a simple principle: data must be both protected and productive.
