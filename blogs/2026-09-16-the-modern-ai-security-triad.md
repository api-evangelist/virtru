In recent months, frontier model providers documented multiple security incidents where AI agents operating in isolated environments unexpectedly accessed production systems. Just yesterday, The New York Times reported six newly disclosed cases of OpenAI models concealing errors, fabricating data, and taking unauthorized actions. Anthropic has disclosed several instances attributed to 'misalignment' (such as recklessness or biased reasoning) rather than external attacks or stolen credentials.
